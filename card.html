三、写作：3 段骨架（照这三句写）

段落作用英文
作文开头第一句：点题：坚持是成功最宝贵的品质之一Persistence is one of the most precious qualities of human beings and an indispensable foundation of success.
第二段开头：举例子：许多例子都证明了坚持的力量Numerous examples have proved the power of persistence.
第二段举例：以学生为例（举一类人）For students, persistent daily study helps us accumulate knowledge steadily and make progress gradually.
第三段第一条建议：如何培养坚持的品质First, we need to set clear short-term and long-term goals to guide our actions.
结尾点睛句：只有坚持才能实现梦想（高分倒装）Only by persisting continuously can we realize our dreams and embrace a better future.

四、原文全文（有余力再背）

Chinese traditional culture has a long history and is extensive and profound.

Reading traditional classics can not only enrich our knowledge reserve and improve our cultural literacy,

but also help us establish correct outlooks on life and values.

In today's rapidly developing era, inheriting and promoting excellent traditional culture is the responsibility and mission of every young person,

and it can also enable Chinese culture to glow with new vitality on the world stage.
