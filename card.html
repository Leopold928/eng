四、原文全文（有余力再背）

翻译全文（5 句）：

Chinese traditional culture has a long history and is extensive and profound.

Reading traditional classics can not only enrich our knowledge and improve our cultural literacy,

but also help us establish a correct outlook on life and values.

In today's rapidly developing era, inheriting and promoting excellent traditional culture is the responsibility and mission of every young person,

and it can also enable Chinese culture to glow with new vitality on the world stage.

作文全文（141 词）：

Persistence is one of the most precious qualities of human beings and an indispensable foundation of success. No one can achieve great goals easily, and all outstanding achievements are accompanied by long-term persistence and unremitting efforts.

第一段（点题）：坚持是人类最宝贵的品质之一，也是成功不可或缺的基础。没有人能轻易实现伟大的目标，所有杰出成就都伴随着长期坚持和不懈努力。

Numerous examples have proved the power of persistence. For students, persistent daily study helps us accumulate knowledge steadily and make progress gradually. For scientists, decades of persistent research enables them to make breakthroughs in scientific fields. Many people fail not because they lack ability, but because they give up halfway.

第二段（举例）：许多例子证明了坚持的力量。对学生来说，坚持每天学习能帮我们稳步积累知识、逐步进步；对科学家来说，数十年的坚持研究让他们在科学领域取得突破。很多人失败不是因为缺少能力，而是因为半途而废。

As college students, we should cultivate the quality of persistence. First, we need to set clear short-term and long-term goals to guide our actions. Second, we should stick to daily plans and never give up easily when facing difficulties. In short, only by persisting continuously can we realize our dreams and embrace a better future.

第三段（结论+建议）：作为大学生，我们应该培养坚持的品质。第一，设定明确的短期和长期目标来指导我们的行动；第二，坚持每天的计划，遇到困难绝不轻易放弃。总之，只有坚持不懈，我们才能实现梦想、拥抱更美好的未来。
